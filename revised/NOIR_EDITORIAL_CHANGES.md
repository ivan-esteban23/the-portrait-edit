# Issue 02 - Noir editorial redesign

The user supplied four Canva portfolio screenshots and requested a similar editorial style for Issue 02 with smaller, more restrained fonts. This is a new design inspired by that reference; no Canva project, template file or licensed template assets were imported.

## Design and typography

- Black pages, white text, light-gray captions, fine rules and Liberation Sans throughout.
- A three-image cover, an image beside the editor's letter, and three-image chapter openers.
- Alternating image-left/image-right study layouts (87 of each), with wider prompt columns and generous margins.
- Prompts: 10.5 pt / 14.5 pt leading; original notes and Try this: 10 pt / 14 pt leading.
- Contents: 10 pt; glossary definitions: 10.5 pt. Study titles: up to 22 pt, chapter headings: 26 pt, cover title: 30 pt. Running headers/footers remain small furniture.
- All references remain uncropped, undistorted and at their original pixel dimensions. The added cover/chapter placements reuse existing sources.
