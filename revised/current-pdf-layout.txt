Autumn Hat Portrait

                                                                               CHATGPT / IMAGE PROMPT



                                                                               A vertical portrait of a young woman with long
                                                                               light-brown hair and freckles, wearing a
                                                                               wide-brimmed black hat with a dark fabric
                                                                               rosette and a high-necked black top with
                                                                               textured lace-like detailing. She glances over
                                                                               her shoulder toward the camera with a calm,
                                                                               slightly serious expression. Shot from mid-thigh
                                                                               up with a shallow depth of field, the
                                                                               background a soft blur of autumn foliage in
                                                                               amber and gold at the top left, fading into deep
                                                                               green. Soft, diffused natural daylight, muted
                                                                               warm-cool palette dominated by blacks and
                                                                               greens, with gentle film grain and vintage 1970s
                                                                               color rendering. Melancholic, romantic,
                                                                               Victorian-inspired mood, cinematic and
                                                                               nostalgic.




REFERENCE / 4916.JPG



Moody, film-like, Victorian-inspired

WORKING NOTES


Original prompt is long; a trimmed version may give more controlled results.
Consider adding a film stock anchor such as "35mm, Kodak Portra."
