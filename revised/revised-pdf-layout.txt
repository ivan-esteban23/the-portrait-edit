Nine chapters bring this collection of 174 studies into focus. Each page places the complete
original prompt beside its linked reference, with the original working notes below. The
glossary introduces recurring photographic and clothing terms.




The prompts were written for DALL-E-style image tools. “Image Prompt” is used throughout
as a neutral label. To explore a study, paste its prompt into your preferred image tool and
use the reference attachment when supported. The added “Try this” line draws only from that
study's existing notes.




Every study number, title, source filename, prompt and working note is preserved. Review pairing
marks six potentially mismatched source links. Repeats Study identifies shared or closely related
references. Rights check asks the publisher to verify likeness and publication permissions; it does not
establish anyone's identity.
